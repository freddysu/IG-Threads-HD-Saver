# Privacy Policy — IG & Threads HD Saver

**Last updated: November 25, 2026**

IG & Threads HD Saver helps users download media from Instagram and Threads pages they are viewing. This policy explains what information the extension handles and how it is used.

## Information the Extension Handles

When you use the extension on Instagram or Threads, it may access:

- Media URLs and related media information from the page you are viewing
- The current page path, to identify the type of post or media
- The creator’s username, which may be used in a downloaded filename
- A media URL and optional audio URL, plus conversion details, when you start a conversion
- Your selected interface language

## How Information Is Used

The extension uses this information only to identify media you choose to download or convert, create filenames, and provide the extension’s features.

Your language preference is saved in Chrome local storage. A conversion job record is temporarily stored in Chrome session storage. The extension removes that record when the converter reads it; session data is also cleared when the browser session ends. Conversion is performed in your browser.

## How Information Is Shared

The extension does not send page content, usernames, media URLs, or language preferences to servers operated by the developer. It does not use analytics or sell this information.

When you download or convert media, Chrome and your browser may request the selected media from Instagram, Threads, or their media delivery domains. Those services handle the requests under their own privacy policies.

The extension includes an optional Ko-fi donation link. If you choose to open it, your visit is handled by Ko-fi under its own privacy policy.

## Information the Extension Does Not Request

The extension does not ask you to provide your Instagram or Threads password.

## Your Choices

You can stop the extension from accessing a page by not using it on that page or by removing the extension. Removing the extension deletes its locally stored language preference. Temporary conversion data is kept in browser session storage as described above.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the extension’s features or data handling practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.
